What is PRICE ProtoSem?

PRICE ProtoSem is an elite innovation fellowship orchestrated by Forge Innovation Ventures, engineered specifically to cultivate next-generation retail leaders, product innovators, and venture builders.

PRICE stands for Phygital Retail and Intelligent Commerce and Entrepreneurship. Rather than keeping students confined within single-discipline academic silos, ProtoSem drives an intentional metamorphosis:

The Core Shift: Moving from I-shaped single-domain specialists to M-shaped multi-domain specialists who can seamlessly cross mechanical design, AI tools, commercial viability, and user psychology.
Cohort: Multidisciplinary Economics + Tech + Design

WEEK 00 Pre-Orientation & Hands-On Prototyping

From Blender Roots to Fusion 360 & Clay Modeling

Aug 12 – Aug 14 • KCT Tech Park

Three days before the official PRICE opening, I reported to KCT Tech Park—the Forge—joining technical immersion sessions held for cross-disciplinary cohorts including PRISM and ASADI.

Session 1: Mechanical Designing & Parametric CAD (Facilitated by Sabarish)

We were introduced to Autodesk Fusion 360. Having previously worked with 3D modeling tools like Blender, navigating the parametric CAD workspace felt intuitive, exciting, and surprisingly versatile.

"In the afternoon, we were asked to choose an image from Pinterest and recreate it using clay. By the time the clay reached me, I had roughly half a bar of pink clay left. I improvised and sculpted an Armoured Personnel Carrier (APC). The next assignment was to recreate the same model digitally in Fusion 360."

The subsequent days moved rapidly into intermediate parametric sketching, solid extrusion operations, geometric constraints, and dimensional part modeling based on technical engineering drawings.

Aerodynamics & Balance Challenge: A 5-minute collaborative paper-airplane sprint with an unfamiliar peer—iterating wing balance, lift, and straight-line trajectory.
#Fusion360 #ClayModeling #RapidPrototyping #ForgeTechPark
WEEK 01 Official Fellowship Launch

From Strangers to a Squad: Breaking the Seal

Aug 17 – Aug 21 • PRICE Cohort
Monday, August 17 • Breaking the Seal Enter the Room as Strangers

We entered the room as strangers. Soon after, we were asked to stand up and introduce ourselves—not just stating our names and departments, but articulating what we were exceptionally good at and what specific value we could contribute to a team. I introduced myself as Parameshwari S., Third Year Economics. In a room full of multidisciplinary talent, it served as a catalyst for deep self-reflection: How do you clearly define your unique value proposition?

Gowri Shankar facilitated an energetic Rock-Paper-Scissors tournament activity. The goal wasn't the game itself; it was an intentional social icebreaker designed to dissolve peer friction and spark cross-department collaboration. By afternoon, we transitioned from socializing to problem formulation, defining three distinct commercial challenges and pitching solutions.

"The message was clear: do not just sit and passively learn. Start thinking as builders."
Tuesday, August 18 • Identity & Storytelling Knowing Yourself Is One Thing. Pitching Yourself Is Another.

Day 2 commenced with a professional branding and LinkedIn onboarding workshop. We refined our digital presence and officially incorporated the title of Innovation Engineer Trainee — Full-time, On-site Internship.

We completed the 16Personalities framework assessment, where I tested as ENFJ-T (The Protagonist). For the Zen Pencils storytelling sprint, I selected Amy Poehler’s narrative: “Great People Do Things Before They’re Ready.”

"I enter the battlefield with my curiosity as my weapon, and it will turn my experience into my armor. Skill is not just theoretical knowledge you claim on paper—it is what you have actively tested under pressure, executed in teams, and refined through feedback."
Wednesday, August 19 • Squad Formation & Rapid Build New Squad & The 41cm Tower

Day 3 marked our transition into official Beta Teams. The squad chemistry clicked immediately, bringing authentic energy and multidisciplinary enthusiasm to the table.

We dissected what makes a compelling Tech Talk—taking complex emerging technologies, distilling core principles, and educating peers (using Forge4Fauj and iDEX as case studies). Later, an interactive game of Among Us challenged our social deduction, observation instincts, and group communication dynamics.

Thursday, August 20 • Contribution & PRICE Inauguration The Longest Night in Gotham: The Art of Prompting

In the afternoon, the official PRICE ProtoSem Inauguration took place with Kumar Rajagopalan, CEO of the Retailers Association of India (RAI). He articulated high-level retail commerce dynamics and introduced the cornerstone framework of modern intelligent commerce:

The PAFE Framework:
  • Personalised
  • Accurate
  • Fast
  • Everywhere
Friday, August 21 • World Entrepreneurs’ Day & Reflections The Leaderboard Chronicles

August 21 coincided with World Entrepreneurs’ Day. We joined an intensive session alongside BBA cohorts participating in a 24-hour innovation sprint.

Distinguished industry leaders Swathi Sree (Founder, A Toddler’s Thing) and Ramakrishnan Muthu (MD, Thulasi Pharmacy India) shared raw, unvarnished insights into entrepreneurial grit, supply chain scaling, and customer obsession.
